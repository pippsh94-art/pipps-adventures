Montana Small-Town Camping Trip — 18ft RV from Star, ID

Yellowstone-to-Glacier town hop with real camps. Built off the Beeloved City small-town route, reordered where geography or an 18ft rig demanded it.

Rig: 18ft self-contained RV
Start / end: Star, Idaho
Default length: 12 nights / 13 days
Compress: 10 days Expand: 14–16 days
Season: late June–mid September (most USFS loops closed or dry outside that)

Drive times are OSRM estimates (loaded camper will be slower). Recheck Recreation.gov / Reserve America the week you book — loops and dump stations change.

The days

Day 1 — Star to Island Park

Why: The only sane way to start. 378 miles. Front-load the interstate, finish on US-20 through Rexburg / Ashton / Island Park.

Do: Leave early. Fuel and a full water tank in Star or Mountain Home. Do not tack on Craters of the Moon unless you split this into two days.

Camp

Name Fit for 18ft Book Blunt
Primary Buffalo Campground (Caribou-Targhee NF) Yes. River sites, vault toilets, water in season. Rec.gov id 232219. ~49 listed sites (FS page also describes a larger complex). Recreation.gov. Late May–early Sept; after that FCFS, no water/toilets. 26 miles south of West Yellowstone. Trout water. $25 single on the FS page.
Backup Henry’s Lake State Park Yes. Closer to the pass. Idaho Parks / Reserve America Dump station closed for the 2025/26 season (drain field). Full-hookup sites still dump. Winter note: limited dry sites. Alternate dumps: Bing Lemke FS (Last Chance) or Grizzly RV in West Yellowstone.

Next drive: 28 mi / 35 min to West Yellowstone.

Days 2–3 — West Yellowstone (two nights)

Why: Blog stop 1. West entrance town. Built for the park. Souvenir street is whatever — Grizzly & Wolf Discovery Center and Museum of the Yellowstone are the actual stops.

Do: Day 2 settle + town. Day 3 Yellowstone west side without relocating the camper (Madison, Fountain Paint Pot, Grand Prismatic overlook). If you want to sleep in the park, that’s a different reservation (see bookings). No car-camping in pullouts — NPS is explicit.

Camp

Name Fit for 18ft Book Blunt
Primary Baker’s Hole (Custer Gallatin NF) Easy. 73 sites, 33 electric, average spur 25×75. No dump, no showers. Recreation.gov id 10231392. FS: $20 / $28 electric (page also lists a higher peak pair). 3 minutes from town on the Madison. This is the right base.
Backup A Rainbow Point (Hebgen Lake) Easy. 83 sites, 46 electric, spur ~60×33. Boat ramp. Recreation.gov id 233131 Better if you want lake over town.
Backup B Lonesomehurst (Hebgen, South Fork Arm) Yes. 26 sites, 4 electric, spur ~30×45. Recreation.gov id 233331 Smaller, quieter, fewer hookups.

Private nuclear option in town: Yellowstone Grizzly RV (hookups, dump, priced like a hotel — confirm current rates). Use it if Baker’s/Rainbow are gone.

Next drive: 71 mi / 1.3h north on US-287 to Ennis.

Days 4–5 — Ennis, with a Virginia City day trip

Why: Blog stops 2–4. Ennis is a working cowboy town (Willie’s Distillery, Gravel Bar, Madison River). Virginia City + Nevada City are a 14-mile / 25-minute pair on the old Vigilante Trail (normal paved road). Leave the camper in Ennis.

Do:
- Day 4: Main Street, fishing, Madison Valley Lookout if you want the view.
- Day 5: Virginia City (National Historic Landmark, Bale of Hay = oldest saloon in MT, summer ghost tours) + Nevada City (3 minutes further, walk the signs). Then back to the same site.

Camp

Name Fit for 18ft Book Blunt
Primary Madison River Campground (Beaverhead-Deerlodge NF) Yes, but only 11 sites. Recreation.gov id 234904 Small. Book it or have the backup ready.
Backup Ennis-area private RV / fishing-access camps (Ennis RV Village and similar) 18ft is not the problem Call / their sites Hookups and a dump when you need one. Confirm length and river-access fees.

Do not make Nevada City a separate camp night.

Next drive: either 113 mi / 2.2h to Anaconda, or skip and run Virginia City → Deer Lodge (126 mi / 2.4h) then on to Seeley.

Day 6 — Anaconda / Lost Creek (optional)

Why: Blog stop 5. Copper town. Skip is correct unless you want the canyon.

Do if you go: Hard & Thistle, Copper Village Museum. The camping reason is Lost Creek State Park — 50-ft falls, limestone cliffs, bighorns on the walls.

Camp

Name Fit for 18ft Book Blunt
Primary Lost Creek State Park Yes, but 23-ft max. You’re under it. 25 sites. FCFS only 6,211 ft. Opens ~mid-May (FWP had it open 7 May 2026). Pack-in/pack-out, vault toilets, water. Tight canyon road — daylight, no heroics.
Backup Skip the night and continue to Deer Lodge / Seeley — — Don’t invent a Georgetown Lake plan unless you want a second optional night.

Next drive: 25 mi / 31 min to Deer Lodge.

Day 7 — Deer Lodge as a stop, Seeley as the bed

Why: Blog stop 6. Author was right: budget real time. Old Montana Prison + Auto Museum (closed as a prison 1979). Grant-Kohrs Ranch NHS — working cattle ranch. Then leave. Seeley is the camp.

Do: Morning prison + ranch. Groceries if needed. Drive I-90 west toward Missoula, then MT-200 / MT-83 to Seeley.

If you must overnight in Deer Lodge

Name Fit for 18ft Book Blunt
Emergency overnight Indian Creek Campground (private, Deer Lodge) Yes. 60 RV sites, pull-throughs, 50-amp, dump, wifi. Their site / phone One mile from the prison. Fine for a hookup/dump night. Not scenery.

Next drive already built in: 94 mi / 1.8h Deer Lodge → Seeley Lake.

Days 7–8 — Seeley Lake (two nights; night 7 is the arrival)

Why: Blog stop 7. The breath. Paddleboard, swim, do nothing. Then the Swan Valley is better when you’re not fried.

Camp

Name Fit for 18ft Book Blunt
Primary Seeley Lake Campground (Lolo NF, west shore) Yes. 29 sites. $20. Host. 4 miles to town groceries. Recreation.gov id 10227380. All reservable. C Loop had a 22 May–25 Jun 2026 road closure on the FS alert — check before you book a C-loop site.
Backup A Big Larch (Lolo NF) Yes. 53 sites / 29 individual RV-tent in season. Boat launch, swim beach. $20. Recreation.gov id 234346. Memorial–Labor Day reservable, then FCFS. Loop 1 year-round if snow allows. Right off MT-83, 1 mile north of town. Easiest in/out.
Backup B River Point Yes. 27 sites. Recreation.gov id 10107638 Quieter neighbor of Seeley Lake CG.
Backup C Salmon Lake State Park Yes. 24 sites, some electric. Reserve America 15 min south. Better if you want hookups.

Bear food-storage order is in force on Lolo NF. Use it.

Next drive: 112 mi / 2.2h up MT-83 to Whitefish. Scenic. Daylight only. Kalispell is the grocery/antique optional pause, not a camp.

Days 9–10 — Whitefish (two nights)

Why: Blog stop 8. Rocky Mountain scenery shift. Walk town. Whitefish Mountain Resort summer lift to Summit House; hike down Danny On Trail if you want.

Camp

Name Fit for 18ft Book Blunt
Primary Whitefish Lake State Park Yes. 40-ft limit, 25 sites, beach, boat launch. montanastateparks.reserveamerica.com Trains. Regularly. Right next to the park. If you’re a light sleeper, this is the wrong primary.
Backup Private Whitefish / Columbia Falls RV parks 18ft is easy Call Use this if the state park is gone or you hate trains. Don’t add a third camp just to be in Columbia Falls.

Next drive: Whitefish → Columbia Falls (9 mi) → Hungry Horse (7 mi) → West Glacier (10 mi). Groceries in Columbia Falls. Huckleberry pie + dam viewpoint in Hungry Horse. Then park the rig for two nights.

Days 11–12 — Glacier west gate (two nights)

Why: Blog stops 9–11 collapsed into one base. West Glacier cafés + Crown of the Continent Discovery Center. Going-to-the-Sun as a truck/toad day, not a camper crossing.

2026 access (NPS): no park-wide vehicle reservation. Logan Pass has timed 3-hour parking and a ticketed shuttle. Read the current Logan Pass page the week you go.

Camp

Name Fit for 18ft Book Blunt
Primary Fish Creek Campground (Glacier NP) Best in-park match. 171 sites. Lake McDonald west side. Recreation.gov id 232493 Book at the 6-month window. This is the one worth planning around.
Backup A Apgar Campground (Glacier NP) 18ft usually yes; 187 sites but tighter, more chaos. Recreation.gov id 10171274 Closer to the village. Harder to live in.
Backup B Private: Glacier Campground / West Glacier KOA-class parks Easy Their sites When Fish Creek is gone, pay the private and stop refreshing Rec.gov at midnight.

Sprague Creek is a no for most RVs (short, no towed units). Hungry Horse Reservoir camps (Lid Creek, Emery Bay, etc.) are a fine overflow if you want water and don’t need to be in the park.

Do: One Logan Pass / GTTS day. One easier day (Apgar village, Lake McDonald, pie you missed). Camper stays put.

Day 13 — Homeward: Lolo / Powell

Why: Don’t do Glacier → Star in one 11-hour camper day.

Route: US-2 / MT-206 / MT-35 / US-93 to Missoula (138 mi / 3.1h). Dump and grocery in Missoula if tanks are ugly. Then US-12 west over Lolo Pass in daylight to Powell.

Camp

Name Fit for 18ft Book Blunt
Primary Powell Campground (Nez Perce-Clearwater NF) Yes. 40-ft. 34 sites, 23 electric, 19 reservable. Paved loops. Half mile off US-12 near mp 162. Recreation.gov (search Powell Campground, Lochsa) 60 mi SW of Missoula. Lochsa Lodge / ranger station next door.
Backup Wilderness Gateway (US-12 mp 122.5) Yes. 50-ft. 91 sites. Some reservable ~40 miles further west. Dump station was closed in 2024 — don’t count on it.
Weather bail Missoula private RV park Easy Call If Lolo is a thunderstorm/snow/fire-closure day, sleep in town and do the pass in the morning.

Next drive: ~320 mi / 6.5–7h. US-12 to Kooskia / Grangeville, US-95 south (White Bird, Riggins canyon, New Meadows, Cascade corridor) → ID-44 to Star.

Day 14 — US-95 to Star

Why: Last miles. 18ft is fine on US-95. Riggins canyon is winding, not a 4x4 road. Daylight. Don’t rush the White Bird grade.

Camp: Home. If you’re cooked at New Meadows / McCall, that’s an optional 15th night (Ponderosa SP books early — you already know that from the Idaho guide).

Two maps in your head

Outbound (easy camper): Star → I-84 east → I-86 → US-20 → Island Park → West Yellowstone → US-287 Ennis → (optional Anaconda) → I-90 Deer Lodge → MT-200/83 Seeley → MT-83 Swan → Whitefish → US-2 Glacier.

Home (fastest OSRM): Glacier → US-93 Missoula → US-12 Lolo → US-95 south → Star.
Not the Coeur d’Alene I-90 loop (that’s longer). Not Going-to-the-Sun with the box on the back.

18ft constraints (non-negotiable)

  • Going-to-the-Sun with the camper: no. Day trip the truck. Logan Pass parking is timed in 2026.
  • Lost Creek: 23-ft limit, FCFS, canyon road, daylight.
  • Lolo Pass / US-12: paved, fine, services are thin. Daylight. Fuel in Missoula.
  • US-95 Riggins: paved, slow, hot in July. The 18ft fits. Your patience might not after 6 hours.
  • No camper on dirt two-tracks for “shortcuts” around Virginia City or Hungry Horse Reservoir spurs you haven’t checked.
  • Bears: grizzly country from Island Park through Glacier. Hard-sided is your friend. Still lock the food, no coolers on the picnic table overnight.
  • Fire: stove is the plan. Rings are a maybe after July.
  • Dumps you can count on: Henry’s Lake dump is down. Baker’s Hole has no dump. Plan Missoula, a private RV park, or a full-hookup night.

Compress / expand

10 days: Day 1 Island Park. Day 2 West Y. Day 3 Ennis + VC same afternoon if you have light. Day 4 Seeley (skip Anaconda, Deer Lodge as a lunch). Day 5 Seeley or push Whitefish. Day 6–7 Glacier. Day 8 Powell. Day 9 Star. That’s tight. No extra YNP.

14–16 days: Second full YNP day. Third Seeley. Third Glacier. Optional McCall/Ponderosa last night. Optional Lost Creek night instead of skipping Anaconda.
